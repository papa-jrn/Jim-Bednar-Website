Where does the money come from?

From you. The county has no sales tax and no income tax to lean on — the county portion of your property tax bill is the backstop for whatever fees, reimbursements, and state and federal dollars do not cover.

The commissioners propose the budget; the County Delegation — the state representatives elected from Sullivan County — reviews and adopts it. That split makes the commissioners’ homework the whole ballgame. A budget built on clear numbers and honest tradeoffs gets scrutinized and passed. One that is not gets fought over, and the taxpayer pays for the delay.
