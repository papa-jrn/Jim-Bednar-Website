What does a county commissioner actually do?

Sullivan County is run day to day by a three-member Board of Commissioners — one seat for each of the county’s three districts. The commissioners are the county’s executive branch: they hire and oversee department heads, manage county property and contracts, and build the annual budget that keeps county services running.

It is unglamorous, hands-on management work: personnel, buildings, insurance, union contracts, capital plans, and a payroll of roughly 280 employees. That is exactly the kind of job Jim is asking to do.
