Sullivan County Health Care

The county’s single largest operation is Sullivan County Health Care in Unity — a 156-bed skilled nursing facility and one of the ten largest nursing homes in New Hampshire. It cares for our parents and grandparents, employs hundreds of our neighbors, and drives a large share of the county budget.

Staffing, Medicaid reimbursement rates, and the cost of an aging building are the pressures every commissioner has to manage. Getting this right is both a moral obligation and the single biggest lever on your county tax bill.
