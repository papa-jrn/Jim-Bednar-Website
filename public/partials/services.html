What else does the county run?

More than most people realize. Alongside the nursing home, Sullivan County operates:

  • ★The Department of Corrections — the county jail and its reentry programs
  • ★The Sheriff’s Office — dispatch, transports, and court security
  • ★The Registry of Deeds — every property record in the county
  • ★Human Services — the county’s share of long-term care costs
  • ★UNH Cooperative Extension — farm, forestry, nutrition, and 4-H programs

All of it spread across 528 square miles, fourteen towns, and the city of Claremont, with the county seat in Newport.
